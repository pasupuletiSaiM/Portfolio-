Graph-based delivery ETA optimization

Finding the network bottlenecks that delay deliveries

Consulting & Analytics Club, Apr 2026

View the analysis
Problem

Delivery time estimates miss the delays that come from structural bottlenecks in a logistics network, so promised times slip and SLAs get breached.

What I did
  • Modeled Delhivery's network as a directed graph from 144K+ trip-segment records using NetworkX.
  • Built a graph-enhanced ETA model and compared it with a baseline.
  • Formulated a counterfactual rule for choosing between FTL and Carting on each leg.
  • Analyzed SLA breaches, revised my centrality ranking after 2 of 3 priority hubs turned out to be misidentified, and flagged the top 3 hubs for upgrade.
Outcome
  • 33% lower error (MAE from 47.1 to 31.5 min)
  • 54.5% of estimates within ±15%, up from baseline
  • FTL saves 120–230 min per leg beyond about 150 km
